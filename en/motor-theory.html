Motorcycle Theory

The basis for safe motorcycling

Motorcycling gives freedom, but also requires extra insight and responsibility in traffic. That's why a good theoretical foundation is important before you start with practical exams and driving lessons.

At WowTheorie we help future motorcyclists to better understand the theory. During the course we pay attention to topics that are specifically important for motorcyclists, such as visual behavior, position on the road, risk perception and traffic insight.

You'll learn how different traffic situations should be assessed and what examiners pay attention to during the theory exam. Through practical examples and clear explanation the theory becomes easier to understand and remember.

Topics that are covered:

  • Traffic rules
  • Position on the road
  • Risk perception
  • Visual technique
  • Right of way rules
  • Traffic insight
  • Responsible traffic behavior

Whether you're just starting out or have taken a theory exam before, our course helps you start your exam better prepared.
