Contact

Do you have questions? Feel free to contact us. We are happy to help you further.

IMPORTANT, DIRECTLY AFTER SIGNING UP

After signing up, you must authorize us immediately via mijn.cbr.nl. Without authorization we cannot reserve an exam for you.

Exam slots are limited and fill up quickly. Don't wait on this.

How to arrange it

  1. 1 Go to mijn.cbr.nl
  2. 2 Log in with your DigiD or the DigiD app
  3. 3 Choose 'Authorize trainer'
  4. 4 Select the correct category
  5. 5 Enter your email address
  6. 6 Choose car, motorcycle or scooter
  7. 7 Enter the trainer code (You will receive this by email from WowTheorie)
  8. 8 Accept the terms
  9. 9 Click on 'Authorize'

Why this is needed

  • We can reserve your exam
  • Your spot at the CBR is secured
  • You avoid delays in your planning

What you need to do:

Arrange this directly after signing up. Without authorization your exam cannot be scheduled.
