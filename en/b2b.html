Partner benefits

What you get as a partner

A B2B partnership with WowTheorie means more than just enrolling students. We take everything off your hands - from planning to reporting.

Favorable partner rates

Volume discount based on the number of students you enroll per period - the more, the better the rate.

Dedicated account manager

One fixed point of contact for all planning questions, enrollments and ongoing matters - always reachable.

Priority enrollment

Your students come first. No waiting times, no hassle - just scheduled quickly on the date you choose.

Resit guarantee

Does a student fail? No extra costs. We arrange a new chance free of charge until they pass.

Centralized invoicing

No separate invoices per student - everything bundled at organization level with clear monthly overviews.

Pass rate reports

Periodic overview of pass results per group or participant - so you always know how it's going.

How it works

Become a partner in 4 steps

From first contact to your first successful students - we guide you step by step.

1

No-obligation conversation

We schedule an introductory meeting to map out your wishes and volume. By phone or on location.

2

Custom quote

Based on your situation you receive a transparent quote with partner rates within one business day.

3

Agreement

After approval you sign a partnership agreement and immediately get access to your account manager.

4

Enroll students

You enroll students. We arrange planning, CBR exam and guidance. You receive the results.

Response time

After your request via the contact page we respond within one business day with a tailored proposal. No obligations - you decide yourself whether and when you start.
