Car Theory

Your first step towards the car driving license

Before you can take your practical exam, you first need to pass the theory exam. At WowTheorie we help you to better understand traffic rules, traffic insights and situations on the road, so that you begin your exam well prepared.

During the course we cover the topics that are important for the car theory exam. You'll learn not only the rules, but also how to apply them in real traffic situations. Through clear explanation and practical examples you gain more insight into what is expected of you during the exam.

Our course is suitable for everyone. Whether you're doing theory for the first time, have already failed once or have trouble learning independently. We provide a clear structure so that you can work in a focused way on your preparation.

What do we cover during the course?

  • Traffic rules
  • Traffic signs and markings
  • Right of way rules
  • Speed and keeping distance
  • Hazard perception
  • Responsible driving behavior
  • Traffic insight

With good preparation you increase your chance of entering the exam with confidence.
