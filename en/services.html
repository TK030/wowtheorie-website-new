1-Day Car Theory Course

At WOW Theorie you follow an intensive 1-day theory course for your car driving licence. You start the course in the morning and take your CBR theory exam that same day.

You learn exactly what you need to pass the exam. No separate chapters or studying at home. Everything is explained step by step with clear examples from real traffic situations.

How the day looks

  • Morning: explanation of all theory topics
  • In between: short practice questions and explanations
  • Afternoon: review of the most important points
  • Afterwards: directly to your CBR theory exam

What you will learn

  • Traffic rules
  • Road signs
  • Right-of-way rules
  • Hazard recognition
  • Speed and distance
  • Traffic insight

Who this course is for

  • You are taking your theory exam for the first time
  • You have failed before
  • You find studying independently difficult
  • You want everything sorted in one day

What you get

  • Course in 1 day
  • Exam on the same day
  • Clear explanation without difficult language
  • Guidance throughout the entire day
  • Full focus on the CBR exam

WOW Theorie provides structure and clarity. You know exactly what is important and what to pay attention to during the exam.

2-Day Car Theory Course

At WOW Theorie you follow a 2-day car theory course. You get extra time to understand and practise the theory. Day 1 is all about explanation. Day 2 is all about revision and preparation for your CBR exam.

This course is suitable if you need more time or if you want to let the material sink in better.

Day 1

  • Explanation of all theory topics
  • Traffic rules and road signs
  • Right-of-way rules and hazard recognition
  • Practice questions during the lesson
  • Immediate explanation of mistakes

Day 2

  • Revision of the most important topics
  • Extra practice questions
  • Focus on weak points
  • Exam training
  • Preparation for the CBR exam

What you will learn

  • Traffic rules
  • Traffic insight
  • Road signs
  • Right-of-way rules
  • Speed and distance
  • Hazard recognition

Who this course is for

  • You struggle with fast-paced learning material
  • You have failed before
  • You want extra time to understand everything
  • You want more practice before your exam

What you get

  • 2 full course days
  • Clear explanation per topic
  • Extra practice moments
  • Personal guidance
  • Focus on exam preparation

WOW Theorie ensures you go through the material step by step. You get time to ask questions and understand your mistakes.
